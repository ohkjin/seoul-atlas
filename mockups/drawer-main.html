Clicking a name moves only the radio — the theme card's headline. The map is the small link icon at the right edge, numbered 1 (colour) and 2 in the order clicked; a third pushes #1 out. To put Retail sales in the lead with RHSI and Max temperature linked: unlink, unlink, link, link.

A · Click the name = main one click shows it; "+ vs" on hover adds the comparison

Clicking a row's name puts it on the map as MAIN (the colour, and what the dashboard follows), replacing the old main; the comparison stays. Hovering a row shows + vs to make it the comparison (height / grid columns). The radio and the link icon go — the theme card's headline follows the theme's main or comparison. Clicking MAIN or VS again takes it off.
